## FAQ

### Do I need technical experience?

No. Some roles require specialized skills, while many program and community roles do not.

### Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes. DAN offers both remote and local opportunities depending on current programs and organizational needs.

### How much time do I need to commit?

Commitment varies by opportunity. Some projects are one-time or event-based, while others involve recurring participation.

### Can students volunteer?

Yes, subject to the requirements of the specific opportunity.

### Can I volunteer from another country?

Remote opportunities may be available globally, while local opportunities depend on DAN's active programs and partners.
